## Macro recorder finally ready! (Aug 13, 2026)

Remember that disabled **RECORD** button we added to the side panel in v14? Well, it finally does something. 😄

We just posted a video tutorial demonstrating the new macro recorder that will be introduced in v15. The idea is pretty simple: click **RECORD**, perform the actions you want to automate, and Lightning Autofill will record each action as a macro step. When you're done, click **STOP**, reload the page, and click **PREVIEW** to play everything back and make sure it works as expected.

In the video, we record ourselves filling out two rich text editors, including entering text, adding headings, moving text around, and applying bold formatting. You can also edit the value or selector of any recorded step before saving it. If there's a step you don't want, click the trash icon to remove it.

Once you're happy with the results, click **SAVE** and the macro will be saved as a regular profile. From that point on, you can execute it just like any other autofill profile.

That said, the web is a wild place, so there's bound to be some sites where the recorder will have a hard time. If you run into one, please let us know so we can take a look.
